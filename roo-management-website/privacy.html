Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Roo Management Inc. ("Roo Management", "we", "us") collects through www.roo-management.com, by phone, by email, and by text message, and how we use it. Questions go to roomanagementtx@gmail.com or (346) 327-0525.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email address, and property address.
  • What you tell us in a form, email, call, or text, such as details about a property or a maintenance request.
  • Your text messaging consent, including the date and the number you opted in with.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you visit the site (for example IP address and browser type), as recorded by our web host.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and prepare a rental analysis.
  • To manage properties and leases, schedule showings, inspections, and repairs, and send account notices.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive (see "Text messaging" below).
  • To meet legal, tax, and fair-housing obligations.

Text messaging

If you opt in to texts, we use your mobile number only to send the messages described in our SMS Terms. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (for example, our phone and text messaging provider, email provider, web host, and property accounting software), with the property owner or resident a request relates to, or when the law requires it. Service providers may use the information only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and lease and accounting records for as long as Texas law and tax rules require.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of text messages.
  • Email roomanagementtx@gmail.com to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted where the law allows.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
